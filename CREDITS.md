## Base

The game began as Prototype 5 ("Clicky Mouse") of Unity Learn's Junior Programmer pathway,
Unit 5 "User Interface". The idea of clicking targets tossed up from below the screen, with a
score, a game-over screen and a difficulty choice, comes from that course.

## Removed content

`Assets/Course Library` (the course's food and skull models, textures, materials, particle
prefabs and fonts), the two course scenes and the six prefabs built on it were removed: Unity
Learn provides that content for following the course, with no licence to redistribute it in a
public repository. The Exo 2 fonts from that folder were kept because they are under the SIL
Open Font License. The 2021 editor logs and per-user settings were untracked. History was not
rewritten.
